# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes a fictional retail sales dataset using Microsoft Excel to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, and explore opportunities for future growth.
![Retail Sales Dashboard](Screenshot%202026-11-10%20182800.png)
## Tools & Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Data visualization
- Business performance analysis

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Revenue:** $29,018
- **Total Gross Profit:** $14,236
- **Units Sold:** 727
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 49.06%

## Key Findings
1. Electronics generated the highest product category revenue at $9,482.
2. August recorded the highest monthly revenue at $3,827.
3. North generated the highest regional revenue ($8,275) and gross profit ($3,885).
4. South achieved the highest regional gross profit margin at 50.62%.

## Business Recommendation
I recommend investigating expansion opportunities in the South region because it has the highest gross profit margin. Although North generates more total profit, South retains a greater percentage of revenue after product costs.

Further analysis of customer demand, operating expenses, and expansion costs would help determine whether additional investment in South is worthwhile.
